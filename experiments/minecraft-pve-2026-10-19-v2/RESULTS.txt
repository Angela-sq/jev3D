TIMING AND INTERPRETATION
Median observation-to-action delay: 9.07 seconds.
Maximum observation-to-action delay: 23.39 seconds.
Median fraction of battle wall time executing inputs: 9.38%.
Median wall/game duration ratio: 0.9989.
Delay includes inference, orchestration and screenshot/tool transport; it is not
pure model inference latency. Inputs release after at most one second, including
movement and shield. The game keeps running during model deliberation. Camera
movement is also bounded (300 pixels per axis per action). These restrictions
materially limit this system. Results do not establish poor spatial reasoning
in Astra, or superiority of Jev. No human, Jev, scripted, no-action or unlimited-
time control is included. Software rendering and host workload can affect timing.
All four timeouts occurred at site B. Terrain can obstruct pursuit as well as
player movement; survival alone is not a victory.
